Support

For people who already use the LittleHQ iPhone app

LittleHQ is for one school. Help starts with the people at that school.

Your school’s records

Message the director in LittleHQ. They can fix invitations, classrooms, attendance, pickup, and charges.

Sign in

  • Use the email the director invited.
  • Forgot your password? Use Reset password. We only email that inbox if it already belongs to LittleHQ.
  • Invitation email: open the link, then sign in on iPhone.

Your account

  • Leave School is in More → Account if you are a teacher or parent.
  • Delete LittleHQ account is in More → Account. You must type DELETE. That removes your sign-in. The school’s shared records stay.

Privacy

Read the Privacy Policy and Terms of Use. LittleHQ does not sell school records and does not use them for ads.
