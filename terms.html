Terms of Use

Last updated August 18, 2026

LittleHQ is a school operations app for directors, teachers, and parents. It is operated by Evernourish Brands. LittleHQ is a placeholder product name. This page describes how the live product is meant to be used. It is not legal advice.

Who may use LittleHQ

LittleHQ is for adults at one childcare school. Children do not create accounts. You need an invitation from that school’s director, or a director inbox the operator has allowlisted. Sign in with email and password in the iPhone app.

The school’s records

The director decides who is invited and what the school stores: children, attendance, pickup, messages, announcements, and tuition. Teachers and parents only see what their role allows. There is no public feed and no posting to people outside that school.

Do not use LittleHQ to impersonate someone, harass a family, or enter records you do not have a right to keep. The director can remove a teacher or parent from the school. Evernourish Brands can close an account that is used to break these terms or to harm a child or family.

Tuition

Charges in LittleHQ are money a family already owes that school. LittleHQ does not sell app content. There are no Apple In-App Purchases. Directors can record cash, check, or Zelle. When a school connects card checkout, card payments are processed by Stripe for that school. The iPhone app never stores a full card number.

Your account

Teachers and parents can leave a school from More → Account → Leave School. Anyone can delete their LittleHQ sign-in from More → Account → Delete LittleHQ account by typing DELETE. Shared school records stay with the school. Read the Privacy Policy.

Questions

For your school’s records, message the director in LittleHQ. For the app itself, use the Support page.
