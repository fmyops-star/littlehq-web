Privacy Policy

Last updated August 16, 2026

LittleHQ is a school operations app for directors, teachers, and parents. It is operated by Evernourish Brands. LittleHQ is a placeholder product name.

This notice describes what the app stores today. It is written to match the live product. It is not legal advice.

Who uses LittleHQ

The app is for adults at a school: the Director, teachers, and parents or guardians. Children do not create their own LittleHQ logins. A Director or parent enters a child’s information so the school can run the day.

What we store

  • Your name, email, and password. The password is stored as a hash by our login provider. We do not keep your password in plain text.
  • School details the Director enters: school name, address, phone, and timezone.
  • Children’s first and last names, classroom, and allergy notes.
  • Attendance (check-in and check-out times).
  • Authorized pickup names and how to reach them.
  • Messages between the Director and a parent about that parent’s child.
  • School announcements.
  • Tuition charges and payments the Director records (for example cash, check, or Zelle). Card checkout is not on yet.
  • Invitations: the invited email and whether the person is a teacher or parent.

Who can see school records

  • The Director can see the school’s people, children, attendance, pickup, messages, announcements, and billing.
  • A teacher can see children in assigned classrooms, attendance, pickup, and announcements. Teachers cannot see billing or Director–parent messages.
  • A parent can see their own children, those children’s attendance and pickup, announcements, messages with the Director, and their own charges.

We do not sell this information. We do not use it for ads.

Where it is stored

LittleHQ uses Supabase to store data and handle email/password sign-in. The live project is in the United States (us-east-2). Invitation, confirmation, and password-reset emails are sent through Resend from LittleHQ at noreply@mail.gatebly.com.

Deleting your account

In the LittleHQ app, open More → Account → Delete LittleHQ account. You must type DELETE and confirm. That removes your sign-in. It does not erase the school’s shared records. Children’s names, allergies, attendance, messages, and payments stay with the school so the school’s history remains complete.

Leaving a school without deleting your sign-in is not a self-serve action. Ask the Director. The Director can also remove someone from the school or delete that person’s LittleHQ sign-in from People.

Children’s information

Children’s names, allergies, and attendance are stored because the school needs them to operate safely. Parents and the Director decide what to enter. If you have a question about a child’s records, start with the Director at that school.

Questions

For your school’s records, contact the Director in LittleHQ. This page is also linked from Sign in and from Account in the app.
